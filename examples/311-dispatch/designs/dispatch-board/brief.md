# Brief: 311 dispatch board

- **User:** borough desk operator (fictional role over real NYC 311 data); expert, uses the screen all shift, overnight included.
- **Primary task:** pick the next open request that needs action and route or escalate it.
- **Decisions:** is this request new work or a duplicate of a known condition; how severe is it (whole building without heat vs. one parked car); how long has it waited; which agency owns it.
- **Data conditions:** 40 requests from a 2.5-hour window (19 open, 14 in progress, 7 closed); 5 open heat complaints are duplicates of a building-wide condition; 15 have no resolution text; 2 have no street; resolution text up to 700 characters; agencies write in different casing (HPD in capitals).
- **Viewports:** 1440 desk; 390 phone for a supervisor away from the desk.
- **Known pain points:** none stated.
